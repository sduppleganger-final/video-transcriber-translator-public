ONE LOCAL WORKSPACE

From moving image
to useful words.

Choose a local video or audio file. The app handles transcription, translation, uploaded-SRT attachment, review, styling, captioned output, and video splitting on your Windows PC or Mac.

01

Transcribe

Create time-aligned subtitles locally with bundled multilingual Whisper, or use the optional Hebrew-optimized Ivrit.ai engine on Windows.

02

Translate

Optionally translate subtitle tracks with bundled local engines, then compare and correct both versions.

03

Split

Break a large video into numbered MP4 parts by estimated length or file size with one simple slider.

04

Attach SRT

Add an existing UTF-8 SRT file to a video, correct and style its cues, and render a new captioned MP4 without changing the source.

IMPORTANT FIRST-LAUNCH INSTRUCTIONS

Opening the Mac app safely

These Mac builds are not signed or notarized by Apple. macOS cannot verify the developer or confirm that Apple checked the app for known malware. Continue only if you downloaded the exact file from this page and trust it.

English

  1. Choose the correct download. In Apple menu  > About This Mac, select Apple Silicon when you see “Chip,” or Intel when you see “Processor” with an Intel name.
  2. Extract the app. Double-click the downloaded ZIP file, then drag Video Transcriber & Translator into your Applications folder.
  3. Try to open it once. Double-click the app in Applications. macOS is expected to block this first attempt because the app is not registered with Apple.
  4. Approve only this app. Open System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll to Security, and click Open Anyway. This option is normally available for about one hour after the blocked attempt.
  5. Confirm the exception. Enter your Mac login password if asked, then click Open. Future launches should work with an ordinary double-click.

Do not disable Gatekeeper globally and do not run Terminal bypass commands. Apple's per-app exception above is the supported route.
