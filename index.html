ONE LOCAL WORKSPACE

From moving image
to useful words.

Choose a local video or audio file. The app handles transcription, translation, review, styling, captioned output, and video splitting on your Windows computer.

01

Transcribe

Create time-aligned subtitles locally with the bundled multilingual Whisper Large v3 Turbo model.

02

Translate

Optionally translate subtitle tracks with bundled local engines, then compare and correct both versions.

03

Split

Break a large video into numbered MP4 parts by estimated length or file size with one simple slider.

REVIEW BEFORE YOU EXPORT

Subtitles you can actually shape.

Correct cue text against the video, switch between original and translated tracks, drag captions into place, and adjust size, text color, and background color before rendering.

  • Captioned MP4 video
  • Timestamped SRT subtitles
  • Readable TXT transcript
  • Optional original and translated outputs

A SIMPLE LOCAL FLOW

Four steps. No upload.

  1. 1Choose local media

    Select a video or audio file such as MP4, MOV, MKV, WAV, MP3, OGG, M4A, AAC, or FLAC.

  2. 2Choose the task

    Transcribe and optionally translate any media, or split a video by estimated length or size.

  3. 3Review the result

    Correct and style subtitles, or let ordered video parts finish automatically.

  4. 4Find it in Downloads

    The finished files are saved directly to your computer with clear names.
